QUESTION: What are the key limitations of Naive RAG systems?

SYSTEM: Baseline RAG
LATENCY: 4.05s
TOKENS_IN: 580

COST_USD: $0.000191

ANSWER:
The key limitations of Naive RAG systems include:

1. **Heavy Reliance on LLMs**: Naive RAG heavily depends on the capabilities of large language models (LLMs), which leads to poor performance when using lightweight models.

2. **Over-Reliance on Augmented Information**: Naive RAG tends to overly rely on augmented information, resulting in outputs that often echo retrieved content without providing insightful or synthesized information.

3. **Indexing Issues**: Naive RAG faces challenges related to indexing, which can affect the retrieval quality.

4. **Limited Reasoning Capabilities in Low Resource Situations**: While Naive RAG retains basic reasoning capabilities in low resource situations, it does not perform as well as more advanced methods.

These limitations prompted the development of Advanced RAG and Modular RAG to address these shortcomings.
