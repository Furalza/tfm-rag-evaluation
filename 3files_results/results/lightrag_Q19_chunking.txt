ANSWER:
The selection of chunk size in Naive RAG systems significantly impacts both retrieval precision and recall. Here’s how:

### 1. **Chunk Size Considerations**
- **Granularity of Retrieval**: The choice between coarse and fine-grained chunk sizes affects the relevance of the retrieved information. Coarser chunks can offer more context, potentially improving precision; however, they might also introduce redundancy, leading to irrelevant information being included in the retrieval process.
- **Noise and Processing Time**: Larger chunks may capture more context but also generate more noise, which requires longer processing time and higher costs. Conversely, smaller chunks might reduce noise but can lead to a loss of essential contextual information.

### 2. **Impact on Precision**
- Precision in retrieval measures the proportion of relevant documents retrieved from the total retrieved documents. 
  - **Smaller Chunks**: When using smaller chunks, the likelihood of retrieving precise, relevant information increases since each chunk is more focused. However, essential contextual information might be lost, leading to potential issues in generating coherent answers.
  - **Larger Chunks**: Larger chunks might lead to retrieving more contextually rich data, improving relevance but can also risk pulling in unrelated information, diminishing overall precision.

### 3. **Impact on Recall**
- Recall assesses the proportion of relevant documents retrieved from the total relevant documents available. 
  - **Smaller Chunks**: These can help ensure that most relevant pieces of information are retrieved, potentially enhancing recall. However, if key information is spread across several smaller chunks, retrieval might miss out on comprehensive context.
  - **Larger Chunks**: While using larger chunks can help ensure that more related content is captured in a single chunk, there’s a risk of missing out on specific relevant details scattered throughout the data, which could negatively affect recall.

### 4. **Chunk Size Evaluation**
To improve retrieval performance in Naive RAG systems, careful evaluation of chunk sizes is crucial. Adjustments in chunk size must aim to balance between maintaining context integrity and minimizing irrelevant information to optimize both precision and recall effectively.

### Summary
In summary, the selection of chunk size in Naive RAG systems directly influences retrieval precision and recall. A thoughtful approach in choosing chunk size, informed by considerations of granularity and relevance, is essential to enhance retrieval outcomes.
